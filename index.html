Oddly Epic Autopilot

A personal automation tool for the YouTube channel Oddly Epic.

Oddly Epic publishes short science stories starring Professor Whiskers, a cartoon cat scientist. This tool prepares the videos and uploads them to the channel owner's own YouTube channel on a schedule. It is used only by the channel owner, with the owner's own Google account.

It uses the YouTube Data API to upload and schedule videos, manage the channel's playlists and description, and read the channel's own statistics and comments.
