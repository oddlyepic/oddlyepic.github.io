Privacy Policy

Oddly Epic Autopilot · Last updated: 8 November 2026

Who uses this app

Oddly Epic Autopilot is a personal tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel "Oddly Epic". It is not offered to the public and it has no other users.

What data the app accesses

After the owner signs in with their Google account and grants permission, the app uses the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API to: upload and schedule videos, edit the channel's description, banner, watermark, playlists and sections, read the channel's own video statistics, and read and reply to comments on the channel's own videos.

How the data is used and stored

  • The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is never sent to anyone except Google's servers.
  • Statistics and comments are processed on the owner's computer to improve future videos. Public comment text may be sent to an AI service (Anthropic's Claude) to pick video ideas; no names or other personal data are required for this.
  • We do not sell, rent or share Google user data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting data and revoking access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the token file on the owner's computer removes the stored access.
